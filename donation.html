Food Safety & Donation Guidelines
Checklist for Donors:
  • Food must be prepared in hygienic environment.
  • Cooked food should be donated within 4-6 hours of cooking.
  • Store cooked food below 5°C or above 60°C.
  • Use clean, sealed food-grade containers.
NGOs will inspect and verify temperature upon pickup.
