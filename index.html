EcoConnect acts as a digital bridge between restaurants with surplus food and nearby verified NGOs. Donors submit details of cooked or packed food, and registered NGOs are notified instantly for swift pickup and distribution.

Any restaurant, caterer, cafeteria, wedding hall, bakery, or individual donor who has prepared surplus, hygienic, and edible food can register and post donations through our platform.

NGOs undergo a verification process during registration to ensure authenticity. Once approved, they receive geographic proximity alerts whenever a food donation is available in their local jurisdiction.

We accept freshly cooked meals, bakery items, packaged dry foods, grains, fruits, and vegetables. All items must comply with safety standards, remain unexpired, and be stored at appropriate temperatures.

When discarded food rots in landfills, it releases methane—a potent greenhouse gas. By diverting edible surplus food to those in need, EcoConnect prevents landfill methane generation and conserves the energy and water used in food production.
